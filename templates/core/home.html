How a rehearsal works

  1. {% icon "sparkles" %}

    1. Brief

    Your prompt is rewritten into a precise research brief: who proposes it, who is affected and how.

  2. {% icon "search" %}

    2. Deep research

    News, Reddit, YouTube comments, public X, Facebook and LinkedIn posts, Wikipedia and your documents.

  3. {% icon "network" %}

    3. Knowledge graph

    Actors, alliances, arguments for and against, facts and rumours, organised for the agents (GraphRAG).

  4. {% icon "users" %}

    4. Population

    Synthetic citizens from real demographics: boda boda riders, students, farmers, civil servants…

  5. {% icon "messages-square" %}

    5. Simulation

    They post, argue, like, share and change their minds on X-, Facebook- and WhatsApp-style platforms.

  6. {% icon "chart-line" %}

    6. Insights

    Who moves and why, which messages persuade or backfire, risks, and a ready-to-share report.
